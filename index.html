A little time together matters

Make the story your own

You can choose any page that fits what your child or family needs in the moment. Let coloring create room for connection without pressure to have every answer.

  1. Choose a page together. What catches your child’s attention today?
  2. Get curious. “What do you think is happening in this picture?”
  3. Notice the Trouble. “What might the Trouble Monster be whispering?”
  4. Find a helpful choice. “Who could help? What small step could the character try?”

A conversation starter: “I can feel mad and still be safe.” Feelings don’t have to disappear before we can accept support and choose what to do next.

Meet the author

Hi, I’m Mike Canfield.

I’m an author, trainer, and Executive Director of Family Success Northwest. Beth and I began our work as house parents in 1983 and were foster parents from 1983 to 2017.

My work brings together storytelling, practical skills, and decades of experience supporting children and families. These books offer another way to talk, learn, and build Personal Power together.

Strong parents build strong communities.
